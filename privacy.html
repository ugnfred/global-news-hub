🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Global News Hub ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Usage Data: Browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and time spent — collected automatically via analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use localStorage to remember your theme preference (dark / light mode). No personally identifiable information is stored locally.
  • Contact Information: Name and email address if you contact us via the contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our news aggregation service.
  • To personalise your browsing experience (theme preference).
  • To analyse site traffic and usage patterns.
  • To respond to your enquiries or feedback.
  • To display relevant advertisements (Google AdSense).

3. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • GNews API — news article data.
  • GoldAPI.io — gold price data.
  • Google Analytics — website analytics.
  • Google AdSense — advertising.
  • Google Fonts — typography.
  • Font Awesome CDN — icons.

4. Cookies

We use minimal first-party storage (localStorage) to save your theme preference. Third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set their own cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry. Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
